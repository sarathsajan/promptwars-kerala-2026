🧠 Quick Crisis De-escalation Protocol (5-4-3-2-1)

  1. 5 Things You See: Look around you and name 5 objects in your sight.
  2. 4 Things You Feel: Touch 4 physical surfaces (e.g. chair, clothes, floor).
  3. 3 Things You Hear: Focus on 3 ambient background sounds.
  4. 2 Things You Smell: Identify 2 distinct scents around you.
  5. 1 Thing You Taste: Take a sip of water or focus on your mouth's taste.
